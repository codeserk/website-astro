Screens and modals now appear with a dithered reveal instead of a fade. It's a `reveal` prop on `View`, and `ScreenView`, `Modal` and the onboarding use it. The whole app is pixel art, a smooth opacity fade was the one thing that looked out of place.

Storage moved to `WatermelonDB` on mobile, and `maestro` runs the flows on a real device.

Small ones: skeletons fade instead of popping, sharing an export goes through the native sheet, and ticking is off by default now. I like the tick, most people apparently find it stressful.
