Moved the secrets and the config to shared [](technology/terraform) resources. Every chart had its own copy of the same values, which was fine with three services and stupid with sixteen. The [](technology/helm) charts got a lot smaller after that.

Then a real bug: `InsertOne` was not setting `TTLHours` and `Bucket`, so single inserts were writing rows that behave differently from the ones written in batch. `InsertMany` got simplified while I was there, and both have tests now.
