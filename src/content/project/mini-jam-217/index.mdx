You are a rock that wished to fly. The wish is granted, on one condition: **keep beating your wings or you come down**.

## May I fly?

One button. That's the whole game.

Every beat you land pushes you higher. The tempo never changes; the question is whether you can keep hitting it. The rock talks on the way up, and something much older talks back.

Made in a weekend for Mini Jam 217, theme _whimsy_. My whimsy is a stone with delusions of grandeur, apparently.

## The technical bit

This one runs on [](/project/simurg), my own [](language/c) engine, so **no Unity, no Godot, no editor**. The whole picture is **one fragment shader** driven by a single `altitude` uniform: near-1-bit through a Bayer dither, no sprites anywhere. The audio too: synthesised in `C`, mixed by the same altitude.
